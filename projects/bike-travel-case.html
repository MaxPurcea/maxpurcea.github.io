Independent project

Ultralight Bicycle Travel Case

Design of a hard-shell bicycle travel case using a contoured carbon/foam sandwich construction. The target mass is approximately half that of the lightest commercially available hard case.

StatusResearch and concept design
Started2026
ToolsFusion 360, SolidWorks, composites
TargetAbout 50% of the lightest commercial case
Objective

Design a hard-shell bicycle travel case with approximately half the mass of the lightest commercial hard case.

My contribution

Independent project: concept, material selection, structural analysis, CAD, and fabrication.

Current stage

Benchmarking and material research, with sandwich-panel coupon testing to follow.

On this page
  1. Concept
  2. Requirements
  3. Plan

Concept

A sandwich panel consists of thin carbon-fiber skins bonded to a lightweight foam core. Bending stiffness increases with the separation between the skins, so a sandwich panel achieves high stiffness at low mass. Contouring the shell to the outline of the bicycle also reduces the enclosed volume compared with a rectangular case.

Figure 1. Sandwich panel cross-section.

Requirements (preliminary)

  • Protect a road or triathlon bicycle during checked-baggage handling.
  • Total mass of approximately half that of the lightest commercial hard case.
  • Remain within standard airline oversize-baggage dimensions.
  • Be manufacturable with composites methods available to students.

Plan

  1. Benchmarking: mass, dimensions, and construction of existing hard and soft cases.
  2. Material selection: skin layups and core materials, sized with sandwich-beam stiffness calculations.
  3. CAD: contoured shell around a reference bicycle envelope, including parting line and hardware.
  4. Coupon testing: flat sandwich panels tested for stiffness and impact response.
  5. Prototype: shell laid up on a foam plug, followed by mass measurement and drop testing.
