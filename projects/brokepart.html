BrokePart: from "it's broken" to fixed

When a tool or appliance breaks, the hard part is often figuring out which part failed and what it's called. BrokePart is a phone app that helps an everyday person identify a broken, faulty, or missing part from a photo and get the thing working again, whether by a DIY fix, a reliable replacement part, or a custom 3D-printed one.

User flow

  1. Capture: a photo and a short description of what's wrong.
  2. Diagnose: identify the system and the likely broken, faulty, or missing part.
  3. Fix it yourself when that's realistic, with clear step-by-step instructions.
  4. Otherwise, source it: affordable, reliable part options, with the reasoning behind each pick.
  5. Special cases, make it: when no part exists, a CAD and 3D-print request for a custom replacement.
  6. Install and operate: a guide to fit the part and get back to normal use.

Decisions so far

  • Expo / React Native for the first version, so one codebase ships to both iPhone and Android.
  • Built around the everyday user, not the repair pro. Plain language, and as few steps as possible between the photo and a fix.
  • The CAD and 3D-print path connects the app to my own engineering work. It's the case where a missing part gets designed, not bought.

Next

  • Get the photo → diagnosis loop working end to end.
  • Test with friends on real broken items, and track how often the diagnosis is right.
  • Add part sourcing, and a simple request form for custom prints.
